["4. (Dadka) baray sida wax loogu qoro qalinka.","Ee Dadka baray sida Qalinka wax loogu qoro."]

["6. Mayee! Aadmigu (kan gaalka ahi) wuu xadgudbaa oo kibraa.","Saas ma aha ee Dadku (Gaaladu) waa gudbeen."]
["7. Markuu isku ogaado Hodantinnimo iyo deeqtoonaan.","Markuu isku ogaaday Hodontinnimo."]
["8. Hubaal! Waa xagga Rabbigaa noqodku.","Eebaha xaggiisaase loo noqon."]
["9. Ka warran midka ka reeba.","Ka warrama midka ka reebaya."]
["10. Addoon (Alle) oo tukan salaad?","Addoon (Eebe) oo tukan Salaadda."]
["11. Bal ka warran hadduu ku sugan yahay (addoonkaa) jid toosan?","Bal ka warrama hadduu ka laga reebi hanuunsanyahay (waa Nabiga)."]
["12. Amaba uu amro Alle ka cabsiga iyo xume ka dhowrsiga?","Ama uu fari Eeba ka yaabid."]
