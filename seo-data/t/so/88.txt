["1. Mawuusan kuu iman warka dabooshada (Qiyaamada).","Ma ku soo gadhay Qiyaamada warkeedii."]
["2. Wejiyaal maalintaas waxay ahaan kuwo dullaysan (waa gaalada).","Wajiyaal Maalintaas way dullaysnaan."]
["3. (Ku) howlan (adduunka caabuddaada cid kale la jirka Alle), daallan (Aakhirada).","Waxayna qaban Shaqo dhib ah."]
["4. Gali Naar kulul oo ku guban.","Waxayna gali Naar kulul."]
["5. Waxaa laga waraabin il kulul oo kareysa.","Waxaana laga waraabin il kulayl daran."]
["6. Ma laha wax cunto ah qodxo maahee.","Wax cunto ahna kama helaan oon ahayn cunno xun (oo wax dhibta)."]
["7. Kaasoo aan waxna cayilineynin, gaajona wax ka tareyn.","Taasoon waxna cayilinaynin Gaajana wax ka taraynin."]
["8. Wejiyo Maalintaas waxay ahaan kuwo nicmeysan (waa wejiyada mu'miniinta).","Wajiyaalna maalintaas way Nicmaysnaan (Waa mu'miniinta)."]
["9. Raalli ah dadaalkoodii darti.","Waxayna ka raalli noqotay camalkeedii."]
["10. Ku sugnaan Janno sare gudaheed.","Janno sarana way gali."]
["11. Aysan ku maqleyn hadal macno darro ah.","Kumana maqasho Jannada hadal macna darro."]
["12. Waxaa ah dhexdeeda il socota.","Waxaana ah dhexdeed il socota."]
["13. Iyo sariirro la kor yeelay.","iyo Sariiro la koryeelay."]
["14. Iyo koobab diyaar laga dhigay.","Iyo koobab la hor dhigay (ehlu Jannaha)."]
["15. Iyo barkimooyin la safay.","Iyo Barkimooyin la safay."]
["16. Iyo gogollo la fidshey.","Iyo gogol la fidiyay."]
["17. Mawaysan fiirin geela, sida loo abuuray?","Miyayna fiirinaynin sida Deela loo abuuray."]
["18. Iyo cirka, sida loo kor yeelay?","Iyo sida Samada loo koryeelay."]
["19. Iyo buuraha, sida loo taagay?","Iyo sida Ruuraha loo taagay,"]
["20. Iyo dhulka, sida loo fidiyey?","Iyo sida dhulka loo fidiyay."]
["21. Ee waani (dadka), waayo waxaad adigu (Nabiyow) uun tahay waaniye.","Ee Waani dadka waxaad tahay waaniyee (Nabiyow)."]
["22. Ma tihid adigu mid qasab ku leh.","Mana tihid mid xoog ku leh (qasab)."]
["23. Qofkiise (Xaqa) ka jeesada oo gaalooba;","Hase yeeshee Ruuxii xaqa ka jeedsada oo Gaalooba (ku soo celi xaqa)."]
["24. Wuxuu Alle ku cadaabi cadaab aad u weyn.","Eebana wuu caddibi Caddibaadda u wayn."]
["25. Hubaal waa Xaggayaga meel noqodkoodu;","Xagganagayna u soo noqon (khalqigu)."]
["26. Markaas hubaal Annagay na saaran tahay xisaabintooda.","Annagaana xisaabin."]
