["1. Mawaadan ogaan sida Rabbigaa u galay qoladii maroodiga?","Ma Ogtahay sida Eeba ku falay asaxaabtii Maroodiga «Colkii»."]
["2. Sow kama uu dhigin dhagartoodii wax dhumay (ku dhammaaday jab iyo jah wareer),","Miyaan Eebe ka yeelin dhagartoodii wax dhumay."]
["3. Oo ku soo dirin shimbiro guutoooyin ah,","Oosan ku dirin korkooda Shimbiro kooxa ah."]
["4. Ku soo ganeysa dhagaxyo dhoobo la kululeeyey ah,","Kuna tuuraysa Dhagaxyo la kululeeyay."]
["5. Markaas ka yeelay sidii bal la daaqay?","Markaas Eebe ka yeelay Bal la daaqay sidiis."]
