["1. Waxaanu xaqiiq soo dejinnay (Qur’aanka) habeenka Qadarka.","Annagaa soo dajinnay Quraanka Habeenka LaylatulQadarka ah (sharafta leh)."]
["2. Maxaa ku ogeysiiyey wuxuu habeenka Qadarku yahay?","Ma taqaanna LaylatulQadarka."]
["3. Habeenka Qadarku wuxuu ka khayr badan yahay kun bilood [1].","Waa Habeen ka khayr badan Kun Bilood (oo kale)."]
["4. Malaa’igta iyo Jabriil baa la soo dega (habeenkaa) Idanka Rabbigood arrin walba oo xukun ah,","Waxaana soo daga Malaa'igta (kale) iyo Malaku Jibriil habecnkaas idanka Eebe dartiis iyo amarkiisa."]
["5. Nabad weeye (habeenkaa) ilaa waagu ka baryo [2].","Waana Habeen nabadgalyo inta waagu ka haryi."]
